AI workflows: first 90 days

Goal: five in production, each with a baseline. Built from COOL's open job posts, Nov 2026.

#WorkflowTeamScore (est.)

Score (est.) = 2 x Impact + Feasibility + Adoption minus Risk. Each is my estimate from 1 to 5.

Impact
How much time or rework it removes, times how often the work happens. Counted twice because it matters most.
Feasibility
Whether the data it needs already sits in tools COOL uses, so it ships without a new system.
Adoption
How likely the team is to use it, based on how closely it fits work they already do.
Risk
What a wrong output could cost before a person catches it. Subtracted.
